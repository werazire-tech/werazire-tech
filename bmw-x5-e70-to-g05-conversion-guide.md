# BMW X5 E70 to G05 Conversion Guide: Fitment, Kit Contents and Installation Checklist

*By TANGMA CRAFTSMAN TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED*

A BMW X5 E70 to G05-style exterior conversion gives an existing E70 a newer appearance through coordinated body panels, bumpers, grilles and lighting.

A successful project starts with three checks: whether the kit matches your vehicle, which components your order includes, and how your workshop will complete installation.

This guide helps vehicle owners and installers prepare before ordering, painting or fitting the kit.

## 1. Confirm Your Vehicle Before Ordering

Send our team the following information:

- Vehicle model and production year
- VIN, shared privately for compatibility checks
- Standard or M Sport exterior configuration
- Destination country and left-hand-drive or right-hand-drive specification
- Clear front, rear and side photographs
- Details of existing headlights, parking sensors, cameras and other fitted equipment
- Details of previous body modifications or accident repairs

Do not select a kit based only on the appearance of a finished conversion photograph. Your vehicle’s mounting points, equipment and electrical configuration must also be checked.

**A G05-style conversion changes the exterior appearance. The vehicle retains its original E70 platform and identity.**

## 3. Review Installation Requirements

### Body panels and mounting points

Your workshop should review the kit and vehicle together before starting work.

Confirm:

- Which factory mounting points are retained
- Which supplied brackets are required
- Whether original trim or supports must be transferred
- Whether any trimming, cutting or welding is required for the confirmed configuration
- How the hood, fenders, bumpers and rear conversion components will align

Obtain vehicle-specific confirmation before treating a conversion as a no-cut or no-weld installation.

### Headlights and electrical connections

Before ordering, provide photographs of the original lamps, connectors and labels where accessible.

Ask our team to confirm:

- Connector compatibility
- Whether adapter harnesses or modules are supplied
- Whether wiring changes or coding are required
- Which original lighting functions are retained
- Whether the lamps suit the destination market’s traffic direction

Treat plug-and-play compatibility as a vehicle-specific confirmation.

### Sensors, cameras and other equipment

Where fitted, check the mounting position and clearance for parking sensors, cameras, radar equipment and headlight washers.

Your installer should identify any equipment that needs adjustment or calibration after installation.

## 4. Installation Checklist

### Before disassembly

- [ ] Confirm the vehicle matches the approved quotation.
- [ ] Photograph the vehicle and record existing damage.
- [ ] Record existing warning lights or diagnostic faults.
- [ ] Inspect delivered parts for shipping damage.
- [ ] Compare all components with the packing list.
- [ ] Confirm that the installer has the relevant fitting references.

### During test fitting

- [ ] Test-fit major panels before painting.
- [ ] Check hood, fender and bumper gaps.
- [ ] Check hood latch engagement and opening clearance.
- [ ] Check headlight and taillight seating.
- [ ] Check wheel-arch and tire clearance.
- [ ] Check rear-panel and tailgate alignment where applicable.
- [ ] Check sensor, camera and washer provisions.
- [ ] Resolve missing brackets or alignment issues before finishing.

**Do not paint first and investigate fitment afterward. Complete the dry fit and resolve any issues before surface preparation.**

### Before final assembly

- [ ] Confirm paint color and finish with the body shop.
- [ ] Test supported lighting functions.
- [ ] Check harness routing and secure connections.
- [ ] Confirm secure mounting of panels and accessories.
- [ ] Check seals, liners and trim.
- [ ] Complete any required coding or calibration.

### Before vehicle handover

- [ ] Check for new warning lights or diagnostic faults.
- [ ] Verify lighting operation and headlight aim.
- [ ] Test parking sensors and cameras where fitted.
- [ ] Check hood and tailgate operation.
- [ ] Have the workshop perform an appropriate final inspection.
- [ ] Keep the quotation, packing list and installation photographs.

## 5. Painting, Budget and Shipping

Plan the project around the complete installed cost.

Ask for separate confirmation of:

- Kit price and selected options
- Freight, insurance and destination charges
- Customs duties and taxes
- Body-shop test fitting and installation
- Painting and surface preparation
- Electrical work, coding or calibration
- Any additional parts identified during inspection

For international shipping, confirm whether the quotation covers delivery to your address or delivery to a destination port. Ask who handles customs clearance, duties, unloading and final delivery.

Use a current written quotation for pricing and delivery estimates.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Will this fit every BMW X5 E70?

Compatibility must be confirmed for the individual vehicle. Send the production year, VIN, equipment details and photographs before ordering.

### Are headlights and taillights included?

They are part of the complete configuration described in this checklist. Confirm their inclusion and specification on your final quotation.

### Does installation require cutting or welding?

Request written confirmation for your exact vehicle and kit configuration. Your installer should review the fitting requirements before starting work.

### Can I paint the kit immediately after delivery?

Complete a professional test fit first. Confirm panel gaps, mounting points, lighting fitment and equipment clearance before painting.

### Does the kit include road-use approval?

Do not assume that a product listing establishes approval in your country. Confirm the documentation and inspection requirements for the destination market before purchase.

### Can I install the kit myself?

Professional body-shop installation is recommended. The project involves panel alignment, paint preparation, lighting checks and potentially equipment calibration.
